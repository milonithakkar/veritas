VERITAS CORP EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Section 7: Leave and Benefits Policy | Version 2.1 | Updated March 2026

7.1 ANNUAL LEAVE
All full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per year.
Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis.
Leave must be approved by your line manager with a minimum of 2 weeks notice.
Unused leave of up to 10 days may be carried over to the next calendar year.

7.2 SICK LEAVE
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
Medical certificates are required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.
Sick leave does not carry over between calendar years.

7.3 PARENTAL LEAVE
Primary carer: 26 weeks at full pay, followed by 26 weeks at statutory pay.
Secondary carer: 4 weeks at full pay.
Adoption leave mirrors maternity leave provisions.
All parental leave is available to employees after 12 months of service.

7.4 REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
Remote work arrangements require line manager approval.
Employees are expected to be available during core hours: 10AM to 4PM local time.
Full remote work (5 days/week) requires HR director approval and is reviewed annually.

7.5 PERFORMANCE REVIEW
Performance reviews are conducted twice per year: June and December.
Reviews assess goals, competencies, and development areas.
Pay adjustments linked to performance are effective from January each year.

7.6 GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE
Employees should raise concerns with their line manager in the first instance.
If unresolved, escalate to HR within 10 working days.
All grievances are treated confidentially.
Retaliation against employees raising grievances is a disciplinary offence.
